Your Qwen Cloud Token Plan gives you monthly credits to access AI models through the API. Each account supports one active Token Plan at a time, and your plan renews automatically every month. Use this page to subscribe, make changes to your plan, or cancel when needed.

## Subscribe to a Token Plan

Choose the plan that matches your expected usage. Qwen Cloud offers three tiers:

| Plan | Price | Monthly credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | \$30/month | 25,000 credits |
| Pro | \$100/month | 100,000 credits |
| Max | \$200/month | 250,000 credits |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the pricing page">
    Visit [qwencloud.com/pricing/token-plan](https://qwencloud.com/pricing/token-plan) and review the available plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a plan">
    Click **Subscribe** under the plan you want. You can only have one active Token Plan at a time, so choose the tier that fits your current needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Enter your payment details and confirm your subscription. Your credits become available immediately after payment is processed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change your plan

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Upgrade to a higher tier">
    Qwen Cloud does not support in-place plan upgrades. To move to a higher tier, you need to cancel your current plan and subscribe to the new one.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Cancel your current plan">
        Log in to [home.qwencloud.com](https://home.qwencloud.com/) and navigate to your billing settings. Cancel your active Token Plan subscription.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Subscribe to the new plan">
        Go to [qwencloud.com/pricing/token-plan](https://qwencloud.com/pricing/token-plan) and subscribe to the Pro or Max plan.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify your new credits">
        After subscribing, confirm your new credit balance in the [Qwen Cloud dashboard](https://home.qwencloud.com/).
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      Your old credits are not carried over when you switch plans. If you have remaining credits on your current plan, use them before canceling.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cancel your subscription">
    You can cancel your Token Plan at any time from your billing settings.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open billing settings">
        Log in to [home.qwencloud.com](https://home.qwencloud.com/) and go to your account billing section.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Cancel your plan">
        Locate your active Token Plan and select **Cancel subscription**. Confirm when prompted.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Note your access period">
        Your credits and API access remain active until the end of the current billing cycle. After that, API calls will stop succeeding.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      Refunds are not available for the Token Plan. Canceling your subscription stops future charges but does not refund any portion of the current billing period.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Manage billing

Visit [home.qwencloud.com](https://home.qwencloud.com/) to manage all aspects of your account, including:

- Viewing your active plan and next renewal date
- Updating your payment method
- Reviewing past invoices
- Canceling or resubscribing to a Token Plan

Your subscription renews on the same date each month. Make sure your payment method is up to date to avoid any interruption in API access.
