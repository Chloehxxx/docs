## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subscribe to a Token Plan">
    Choose a Standard, Pro, or Max plan at [qwencloud.com](https://home.qwencloud.com/) and complete your subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your API credentials">
    After subscribing, copy your API key from the Qwen Cloud dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your AI tool">
    Configure your preferred coding assistant—Qwen Code, Cursor, Cline, Claude Code, or any OpenAI-compatible tool—with your Qwen Cloud credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>
