Qwen Cloud's Token Plan is a monthly subscription that gives you a fixed pool of credits to spend across all supported AI models. Subscribing always costs at least 40% less than buying equivalent capacity at pay-as-you-go rates, so the more you use AI in your workflow, the more you save.

<Note>
  All Token Plan tiers save you **at least 40%** compared to pay-as-you-go pricing on the same models. The discount applies across Qwen3.6-Plus, GLM-5, MiniMax-M2.5, and DeepSeek-V3.2.
</Note>

<Warning>
  You can hold **one active Token Plan subscription per account** at a time. To switch tiers, cancel your current plan and subscribe to the new one. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
</Warning>
